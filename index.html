Every month, Assam's young leave home to build the rest of India.

The Invisible Workforce

Assam cannot protect a workforce it cannot see.

There is no database of Assam's outbound workers. They leave without informing anyone. The state learns of them only after abuse, accidents — or death.

→Ennore, Oct 2025 — 9 Assamese workers died in an accident at an SEZ power-station site.
→Tamil Nadu, 2026 — 2 Assamese women among 9 killed in an ammonia-gas leak at a seafood unit.
→Arunachal, 2026 — 7 Mising youths rescued from starvation and abuse at a construction site.
Today the state brings home the bodies. The care comes after death. It should come before.

The Trap

Unskilled, unverified, unprotected.

Most Assamese migrants enter as helpers and stay there — at the bottom of the wage ladder, with no papers and no safety net.

→Stuck at the bottom. 81% of India's construction workforce is unskilled — the exact bracket Assamese enter as helpers.
→Low wages, locked in. Assam tea wages are ₹280/day — among India's lowest — pushing youth out into more of the same.
→Welfare doesn't travel. Benefits are state-registered; nothing follows a worker across the border.

The Readiness Gap

The plants are coming. Skill for them the right way.

India's experience is consistent — state skilling built without live employer demand produces certificates, not jobs. Karnataka's own audit found just 18% of trainees placed against a 70% target, courses chosen without vacancy evidence.

Assam can leapfrog that mistake: wire skilling to the named, dated demand of Jagiroad, the refineries and the logistics parks — before they switch on.

BetterPlace's model is the inversion: demand first, placement verified, paid on outcomes.

Role-level demand mapping

Cleanroom & assembly operators, industrial electricians, welders, warehouse associates, hospitality staff — the exact roles the pipeline hires, mapped district by district.

Women-first pipelines

Electronics assembly is a proven women's-employment engine across Asia. Assam can design women-first tracks into Jagiroad from day one.

Timing is everything

Skilling has a lead time. Plants hiring in 12–24 months need sourcing & training started now.

Sources & Notes

The evidence behind this proposal.

  • CM Dr. Himanta Biswa Sarma — "≈50 young men die every month after leaving home for work"; "children of Assam should not have to leave for work." Assam Tribune, Feb 2026. Re-elected for a 2nd term (2026 Assam election).

  • Kerala construction split — 56.5% helpers, 23.5% masons, ~20% skilled (survey). Perumbavoor plywood hub (1.5 lakh+ migrants) runs on Assamese labour. IJSSHR; Deccan Herald.
  • Assam labour market — LFPR 66.9% (PLFS 2023–24); 21.16 lakh registered educated job-seekers (2024); 15.4 lakh registered unemployed (Jun 2026); dept. admits no in-state placement scheme. Assam Econ. Survey; Sentinel Assam.
  • The trap — 81% of India's construction workforce unskilled (Knight Frank, 2023); Assam tea wage ₹280/day; wage ladder ₹525→₹605 with certification.
  • Human cost — Ennore (9 deaths, Oct 2025); ammonia-leak deaths (2026); Mising youths rescued from abuse (2026). Assam = NE's top trafficking source state (NCRB). Assam Tribune; EastMojo.
  • Government response — Shraddhanjali scheme (Oct 2025): 354 bodies repatriated by 2026 — reactive, after death. Govt. of Assam.
  • The investment wave — Advantage Assam 2.0 (Feb 2025): ₹4.91 lakh Cr of commitments; Tata Electronics ₹27,000 Cr semiconductor OSAT at Jagiroad (~27,000 direct+indirect jobs projected, media-reported). Govt. of Assam; Tata Electronics.
  • The skilling lesson — Karnataka CAG Report No. 6 of 2025 (CMKKY): 18% placement vs 70% target — the national case for demand-first skilling.
  • Schemes to build on — ASDM, Assam Skill University (Mangaldai), Orunodoi, CM's Atmanirbhar Asom self-employment scheme, CM Flight Scheme (global placement).
  • BetterPlace company figures — present in 500+ cities; 30M+ workers, 2.5M+ skilled. Company-reported.

Migration "scale" figures are estimates or administrative floors — Assam keeps no census of its outbound workers. Incident tolls are from single-outlet reports; verify each before publication. Company figures are company-reported. Year-3 outcomes are targets.
